# 4-Day Gym Routine – V-Taper & Posture Focus

This is a 4-day gym plan optimized for a skinny individual to build a strong **V-taper** (broad shoulders, wide back, filled-out chest and arms), improve **posture**, add **neck strength**, and subtly improve **hips and midsection tightness** for better clothes fit — without widening the waist or hurting upper-body recovery.

---

## Weekly Split (4 Gym Days)

- **Monday** — Push A (Shoulders + Upper Chest + Triceps)
- **Tuesday** — Pull (Back + Rear Delts + Posture + Tummy)
- **Thursday** — Push B (Shoulders/Chest Variation + Triceps + Neck + Tummy)
- **Friday** — Legs + Light Pull/Posture + Hips

**Rest / Light Activity:**  
Wednesday, Saturday, Sunday (walking, stretching, mobility)

---

## General Guidelines

- **Session Duration:** 60–75 minutes
- **Warm-Up:**  
  - 5–10 min light cardio  
  - Dynamic stretches  
  - 1–2 light ramp-up sets of first exercise
- **Sets & Reps:**  
  - 3–4 working sets (unless noted)  
  - 8–12 reps for compounds  
  - 10–20 reps for isolation and posture work  
  - Last set close to failure
- **Rest Times:**  
  - Compounds: 2–3 minutes  
  - Isolation/Posture: 60–90 seconds
- **Progression:**  
  - Add reps or weight every 1–2 weeks
- **Nutrition:**  
  - 300–500 kcal surplus  
  - 1.6–2.2 g protein per kg bodyweight
- **Recovery:**  
  - 7–9 hours sleep  
  - Deload every 6–8 weeks
- **Form Priority:**  
  - Controlled reps  
  - Full range of motion  
  - Prioritize shoulders and back while fresh

---

## Monday — Push A  
**(Shoulders + Upper Chest + Triceps)**

- Overhead Press (barbell or seated dumbbell)  
  **4 × 8–12**
- Incline Barbell or Dumbbell Press  
  **4 × 8–12**
- Lateral Raises (dumbbell or cable)  
  **3 × 10–15**
- High-to-Low Cable Flyes or Incline Dumbbell Flyes  
  **3 × 12–15**
- Close-Grip Bench Press or Overhead Tricep Extension  
  **3 × 10–12**
- Shrugs (dumbbell or barbell)  
  **3 × 10–15**

---

## Tuesday — Pull  
**(Back + Rear Delts + Posture + Tummy)**

- Pull-Ups or Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown  
  **4 × 8–12**
- Chest-Supported Row or Seal Row  
  **4 × 8–12**
- Face Pulls (cable or band)  
  **4 × 12–20**
- Rear Delt Flyes (dumbbell or reverse pec deck)  
  **3 × 12–15**
- Barbell or EZ-Bar Biceps Curl  
  **3 × 10–12**
- Hammer Curls  
  **3 × 12–15**
- Stomach Vacuums  
  **3–4 × 20–30 sec holds**

*Cue:* Exhale fully and pull the belly button toward the spine to tighten the deep core.

---

## Thursday — Push B  
**(Shoulders/Chest Variation + Triceps + Neck + Tummy)**

- Dumbbell Shoulder Press (seated or standing)  
  **4 × 8–12**
- Flat or Slight Incline Dumbbell Press  
  **4 × 8–12**
- Upright Rows **or** Extra Lateral Raises  
  **3 × 10–15**
- Overhead Tricep Extension (cable or dumbbell)  
  **3 × 10–12**
- Dips (chest-forward lean) **or** Close-Grip Push-Ups  
  **3 × max reps**
- Neck Flexion / Extension (plate, harness, or manual)  
  **3 × 15–20**
- Ab Wheel Rollouts **or** Kneeling Cable Crunches  
  **3 × 12–15**

*Focus:* Anti-extension work for a tighter-looking midsection.

---

## Friday — Legs + Light Pull/Posture + Hips

- Romanian Deadlift **or** Trap-Bar Deadlift  
  **3–4 × 8–10**
- Hip Thrusts **or** Glute Bridges (barbell or elevated bodyweight)  
  **3–4 × 10–15**

*Purpose:* Improves posture and fills out pants seat.

- Squats (back squat or goblet) **or** Leg Press  
  **3 × 10–12**

*Cue:* Drive through heels for glute tie-in.

- Leg Curls **or** Glute-Ham Raises  
  **3 × 10–15**
- Face Pulls **or** Banded Pull-Aparts  
  **3 × 15–20**
- Farmer’s Carries  
  **3–4 walks × 30–40 m (heavy)**

*Purpose:* Forced upright posture, traps, and core stability.

---

## Program Notes

- Run this program for **8–12 weeks**
- Shoulders are trained **3–4× per week**
- Back and posture receive heavy emphasis
- Arms, traps, and neck are trained consistently
- Core is tightened using vacuums and anti-extension work
- Hips are subtly developed without waist thickening

**Expected Outcome:**  
Shirts fill out up top, waist appears smaller, pants fit better in the seat and thighs, and posture improves noticeably.
